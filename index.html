Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

Clockky · Last updated: August 15, 2026

Clockky is built on a simple principle: we never connect to your bank account, credit card, or any financial institution. You are always in control of what you share with us.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Clockky collects, how it is used, and your rights as a user.

Information We Collect

Information you provide directly:

  • Subscription names, amounts, billing cycles, and renewal dates you enter manually
  • Custom reminder preferences you configure

Account information:

  • When you sign in with Apple, we receive a unique user identifier from Apple. We do not receive your name or email address unless you choose to share them.

We do not collect:

  • Bank account or credit card information
  • Transaction history or financial records
  • Location data
  • Contacts or calendar data
  • Advertising identifiers

How We Use Your Information

  • To store and sync your subscription data across your devices via Supabase (our cloud database provider)
  • To send local push notifications for upcoming renewals (processed entirely on your device)
  • To process in-app subscription purchases via RevenueCat and Apple's App Store

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Third-Party Services

Clockky uses the following third-party services:

  • Supabase — Cloud database and authentication. Your subscription data is stored securely on Supabase servers. Supabase Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat — In-app purchase management. RevenueCat processes purchase receipts to verify your subscription status. RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • Apple Sign in with Apple — Authentication. Governed by Apple's privacy policy.

None of these services receive your bank account information or financial institution credentials.

Data Storage and Security

Your subscription data is stored in Supabase's secure cloud infrastructure. All data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections. Row-level security ensures that each user can only access their own data.

Renewal reminder notifications are processed entirely on your device using iOS local notifications — no notification content is sent to our servers.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at the email address below. We will delete your data within 30 days of your request.

You can also delete individual subscriptions directly within the app at any time.

Children's Privacy

Clockky is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.
